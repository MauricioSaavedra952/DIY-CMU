Problem Set 7: The Caledonian Conjunx

Description:

As the final project, the task was designing a product of value, functionality, or design. Inspired by the rich heritage of Scotland, The Caledonian Conjunx offers a sophisticated and secure solution for carrying two pairs of your precious eyewear. Whether it's your everyday spectacles and stylish sunglasses, or reading glasses and blue-light blockers, this elegantly designed case ensures they are always protected and within easy reach.

 

Product Features:

  • Dual compartment for glasses or shades
  • MacLeod tartan inspired design
  • Acrylic Celtic knot adornment
  • Priority lies in being compact and portable
  • Made from PLA plastic and acrylic
